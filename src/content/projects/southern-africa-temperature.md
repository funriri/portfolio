## The challenge
For the Pale Blue Dot competition, I explored how free and open-source data could help address a problem connected to the Sustainable Development Goals. My project focused on temperature variation across Southern Africa’s croplands.

## My contribution
I created a map comparing cropland land surface temperatures in 2023 with those in 2000, together with a country-level chart comparing 2023 with 2000, 2010, and 2020.

The submitted poster cites MODIS land surface temperature and SPAM agriculture data. The processing platform will be confirmed before publication.

## What the map shows
The poster describes higher land surface temperatures in inland areas relative to coastal regions for the comparison shown. Its map uses a blue-to-red scale to show temperature differences in cropland areas.

These are comparisons between selected years; they should not on their own be interpreted as a continuous long-term trend or as a measurement of crop yield impacts.
