## The problem
Small-scale farmers in Africa need advice tailored to their crops and growing conditions. Virtual Agronomist AI Chatbot is being developed to support nutrient recommendations and crop planning from the beginning of the season through to the end — acting as a digital agronomist throughout the growing cycle.

## My contribution
I helped initiate the chatbot’s development and lead the work on three sets of tools:

- **Weather tools:** weather-based advice using Google WeatherNext data to support farmers in timing farm activities.
- **Know Your Soil tools:** soil information to inform tailored nutrient advice.
- **Plant health tools:** support for plant health questions during the growing season.

I also develop and maintain the chatbot’s Python scripts and PostgreSQL database workflows, and troubleshoot application issues as part of my work at Innovative Solutions for Decision Agriculture (iSDA).

## Progress so far
Development is ongoing. My current contribution centers on getting the chatbot started and leading these tools. Results and impact will be added as the project progresses and evidence becomes available.
